# Repository instructions

## Experiment writeups

Every experiment README, notebook, and report must be self-contained. A reader
must be able to understand the question, state and circuit definitions,
compared methods, objective, metrics, selection rule, timing convention,
statistical summaries, assumptions, and limitations without opening the source
code.

Use simplified technical English. Define every symbol and specialized term at
its first use. Spell out labels such as a target state, circuit depth, cutoff,
packet budget, restart, exact rescore, and fidelity instead of assuming that the
reader knows what they mean. Include explicit mathematical formulas for state
representations and metrics. When a method has several algorithmic steps,
include short pseudocode. Clearly separate training approximations from final
reported quantities and distinguish computer wall time from physical gate time.

Do not make headline claims from a plot alone. State how many samples or
restarts produced each statistic, how winners were selected, whether hardware
was matched, and which caveats limit the comparison.
