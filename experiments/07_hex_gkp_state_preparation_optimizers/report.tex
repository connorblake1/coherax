\textbf{Target, initial state, and one layer.}
The target is the logical-zero finite-energy hexagonal
Gottesman--Kitaev--Preskill (GKP) state with envelope $\Delta=0.2$. Define
$\alpha=\sqrt{\pi/\sqrt3}$ and
$\eta=e^{2\pi i/3}\alpha$. We retain every integer pair satisfying
$\max(|m|,|l|,|m-l|)\leq14$ and even $m$. With $k=m/2$ and
$d_{ml}=m\alpha+l\eta$, the normalized target is
\[
 \ket G=\mathcal N^{-1/2}\sum_{m,l}
 e^{-i\pi kl-\Delta^2|d_{ml}|^2}\ket{d_{ml}},\quad
 \mathcal N=\sum_{s,t}\widetilde c_s^*\widetilde c_t\langle d_s|d_t\rangle,
\]
where
$\langle a|b\rangle=\exp[-|a-b|^2/2+i(\Re a\Im b-\Im a\Re b)]$.
The sum has 323 coherent sites. Its squared overlap with the radius-20 shell is
$0.9999999999877$, and its 256-level mean photon number is $12.2616$.

Every circuit starts from $\ket0_q\ket0_o$. An echoed conditional displacement
(ECD) moves the oscillator in opposite directions for the two qubit values.
Depth $n\in\{4,8,12,16,20\}$ means $n$ ECD-plus-qubit-rotation layers. A saved row
is $[\Re\beta,\Im\beta,\phi,\theta,\gamma=0]$; the output branches are
displaced by $-\beta/2$ and $+\beta/2$. Each method uses the same three starts
at fixed depth and 10,000 Adam updates per start. The requested initialization
is $\beta\sim\mathcal{CN}(0,0.5)$, meaning
$\Re\beta,\Im\beta\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}\mathcal N(0,0.25)$, with
$\phi,\theta\stackrel{\rm iid}{\sim}U[0,2\pi]$. Adam is the adaptive gradient
optimizer used for every 10,000-update search; ``iid'' means independent and
identically distributed.

\textbf{Five method definitions.}
Full coherent paths keeps all $2^n$ analytic paths and no oscillator cutoff.
Analytic Fock keeps two 48-entry vectors and builds the upper-left block of the
infinite displacement with
\[
D(a)\ket0=\ket a,\qquad
D(a)\ket r=(\hat a^\dagger-a^*)D(a)\ket{r-1}/\sqrt r.
\]
Dense numerical Fock first truncates
$a_{48}=\sum_{r=1}^{47}\sqrt r\ket{r-1}\!\bra r$, forms
$D_{48}^{\rm num}(a)=\exp(a a_{48}^\dagger-a^*a_{48})$, and materializes each
$96\times96$ joint layer unitary. The former is a projected infinite unitary;
the latter is unitary on an artificial finite grid. Dense matrix exponentials
and state propagation use complex64; common final rescoring uses float64.

Continuous anchors and squeezed packets retain 32 packets per qubit branch
plus two 48-entry working Fock arrays (160 propagated complex coefficients).
Residual pivots seed centers, 12 inner Adam steps move them, and coefficients
are globally refit as
$x=(B^\dagger B+10^{-9}I)^{-1}B^\dagger v$. Squeezed packets add 16 steps to
fit one bounded complex squeeze per packet. Both use a heuristic
straight-through derivative for the discrete projection.

\textbf{Training score versus reported score.}
The Fock basis is the photon-number basis $\ket0,\ket1,\ldots$; a 48-level
vector stores photon numbers 0 through 47.
All methods train against the normalized 48-level target projection, which
retains $\langle G|P_{48}|G\rangle=0.9673590488$. This shared approximation
makes the depth-20 search practical; the full coherent circuit state itself is
still untruncated. The cutoff matches the packet working grid while keeping
dense 10,000-step restarts feasible. Every restart is
finally ranked by the cutoff-free score
\[
F_{\rm exact}=\sum_{q=0}^1\left|\sum_j a_{qj}\langle G|z_{qj}\rangle\right|^2,
\quad
\ket\Psi=\sum_q\ket q\sum_j a_{qj}\ket{z_{qj}}.
\]
The qubit is traced out. $1-F_{\rm exact}$ is the reported infidelity.
``Native fidelity'' in the data is a method's internal 48-level score; it is
diagnostic only and never selects the reported winner.

\textbf{Computer search time and physical gate time.}
Wall time measures optimization on this machine. Physical gate time estimates
execution of the saved circuit. The Coherax convention and the separately
labelled Eickbusch-hardware lower bound are
\[
T_C=\sum_j\left[24\,{\rm ns}+\max\left(
\frac{|\beta_j|}{(2\pi\,50\,{\rm kHz})20},48\,{\rm ns}\right)\right],
\]
\[
T_{E,{\rm LB}}=\sum_j\max\left(
\frac{|\beta_j|}{(2\pi\,33\,{\rm kHz})30},224\,{\rm ns}\right).
\]
The repository cannot compile new arbitrary sequences with the upstream pulse
compiler, so $T_{E,{\rm LB}}$ is not a compiled waveform duration. Its 224 ns
floor is two 24 ns qubit pulses plus four 44 ns displacement pulses.

\textbf{Observed result and caveats.}
% RESULTS_DISCUSSION

The 48-level target omits $3.2641\%$ of the exact target norm, so this study is
a comparison of methods under a common practical training objective, followed
by exact ranking. The dense numerical method also has finite-grid boundary
physics and complex64 propagation error. Packet gradients are heuristic. The
independent 256-level validation quantifies the separate error made only when
the exact coherent result is converted for checking. These are best-observed
circuits, not proofs of global optimality.
